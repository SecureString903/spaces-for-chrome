Welcome to Spaces

One clean window per context — work, personal, projects. Your current window is already saved as the General Space.

1

Everything lives in the side panel

Click the Spaces icon in the toolbar to open it (pin the icon for one-click access). Create a Space for each context, then click a Space to switch — your tabs are saved and swapped in place.

2

Switch from the keyboard

Ctrl+Shift+. jumps to the next Space, Ctrl+Shift+, to the previous. Customize both at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

3

Each Space has its own bookmarks bar

Switching Spaces also swaps the bookmarks bar. Bookmarks are moved into hidden folders under Other Bookmarks → Spaces — never copied, never deleted. Press Ctrl+Shift+B if your bar is hidden.

4

Your tabs are never lost

The active Space saves itself continuously, and every switch keeps a backup of what it replaced. The Backups section at the bottom of the panel can reopen any of them.
